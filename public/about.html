Our story

Food that brings Kenya together

One brand, many kitchens. TasteHub connects neighbourhoods to the food they love — cooked fresh, priced fairly and delivered with care.

Who we are

TasteHub Kenya is a multi-branch restaurant group built around a simple idea: great food should be easy to get, wherever you are. Our headquarters in Nairobi supports each branch with shared recipes, training and systems, while every branch keeps its own identity and knows its regulars by name.

When you place an order, it belongs to one branch from start to finish. That branch prepares your meal, packs it, and — if you choose delivery — hands it to one of our drivers. You will get a secure link when your food arrives so you can confirm everything is right.

What we stand for

🍲

Fresh, every day

We plan portions daily and prepare meals to order, so what you get is cooked for you — not reheated from a queue.

Branch first

Each branch sets its own availability, fees and opening hours. Ordering from your local kitchen keeps the service personal.

💰

Honest value

Clear menu prices, simple offers and no surprises at the door. Promotions are applied before you pay.

🛒

We follow through

From the kitchen to your doorstep, our teams stay in touch. If something is wrong, your report goes straight to headquarters and your branch.
